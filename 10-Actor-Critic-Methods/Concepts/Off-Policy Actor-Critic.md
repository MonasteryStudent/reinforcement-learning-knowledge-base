## Definition

**Off-policy actor-critic** learns a target policy

$$
\pi(a\mid s,\boldsymbol{\theta})
$$

from experience generated by a different behavior policy

$$
\beta(a\mid s).
$$

The behavior policy determines how the agent interacts with the environment, while the target policy is the policy being evaluated and improved.

Because actions sampled from $\beta$ generally have different probabilities under $\pi$, [[Importance Sampling]] is used to correct the difference between the two policies.

Both the actor and the critic are trained from off-policy experience. The importance weight is therefore included in both parameter updates.

## Mathematical Formulation

The importance weight for the sampled action $a_t$ is

$$
\frac{
\pi(a_t\mid s_t,\boldsymbol{\theta}_t)
}{
\beta(a_t\mid s_t)
}.
$$

The behavior policy must provide coverage of the target policy:

$$
\beta(a\mid s)>0
\quad
\text{whenever}
\quad
\pi(a\mid s,\boldsymbol{\theta})>0.
$$

The off-policy policy gradient can be expressed as

$$
\nabla_{\boldsymbol{\theta}}J(\boldsymbol{\theta})
=
\mathbb{E}_{S\sim\rho,\ A\sim\beta}
\left[
\frac{
\pi(A\mid S,\boldsymbol{\theta})
}{
\beta(A\mid S)
}
\nabla_{\boldsymbol{\theta}}
\ln\pi(A\mid S,\boldsymbol{\theta})
q_\pi(S,A)
\right],
$$

where $\rho$ is the state distribution associated with the off-policy objective.

Using the state value as a baseline gives

$$
\nabla_{\boldsymbol{\theta}}J(\boldsymbol{\theta})
=
\mathbb{E}
\left[
\frac{
\pi(A\mid S,\boldsymbol{\theta})
}{
\beta(A\mid S)
}
\nabla_{\boldsymbol{\theta}}
\ln\pi(A\mid S,\boldsymbol{\theta})
\left(
q_\pi(S,A)-v_\pi(S)
\right)
\right].
$$

As in [[Advantage Actor-Critic]], the advantage is approximated by the TD error

$$
\delta_t
=
r_{t+1}
+
\gamma v(s_{t+1},\mathbf{w}_t)
-
v(s_t,\mathbf{w}_t).
$$

The actor update is

$$
\boldsymbol{\theta}_{t+1}
=
\boldsymbol{\theta}_t
+
\alpha_\theta
\frac{
\pi(a_t\mid s_t,\boldsymbol{\theta}_t)
}{
\beta(a_t\mid s_t)
}
\delta_t
\nabla_{\boldsymbol{\theta}}
\ln\pi(a_t\mid s_t,\boldsymbol{\theta}_t).
$$

The critic update is

$$
\mathbf{w}_{t+1}
=
\mathbf{w}_t
+
\alpha_w
\frac{
\pi(a_t\mid s_t,\boldsymbol{\theta}_t)
}{
\beta(a_t\mid s_t)
}
\delta_t
\nabla_{\mathbf{w}}
v(s_t,\mathbf{w}_t).
$$

## Algorithm

Initialize the behavior policy $\beta$, target-policy parameters $\boldsymbol{\theta}_0$, state-value parameters $\mathbf{w}_0$, and step sizes $\alpha_\theta,\alpha_w>0$.

At every time step:

1. Sample an action from the behavior policy:

   $$
   a_t
   \sim
   \beta(\cdot\mid s_t).
   $$

2. Execute $a_t$ and observe $r_{t+1}$ and $s_{t+1}$.
3. Calculate the TD error:

   $$
   \delta_t
   =
   r_{t+1}
   +
   \gamma v(s_{t+1},\mathbf{w}_t)
   -
   v(s_t,\mathbf{w}_t).
   $$

4. Calculate the importance weight:

   $$
   \frac{
   \pi(a_t\mid s_t,\boldsymbol{\theta}_t)
   }{
   \beta(a_t\mid s_t)
   }.
   $$

5. Update the actor using the importance-weighted TD error.
6. Update the critic using the same importance-weighted TD error.
7. Continue with state $s_{t+1}$.
